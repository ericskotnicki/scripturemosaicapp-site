Scripture Mosaic Support

Help with the app, purchases, backup and restore, translations, notifications, or feedback.

Email support

Report a problem

Describe what happened, what you expected, your iPhone model, iOS version, and steps that reproduce the issue. Screenshots can help when appropriate.

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased Scripture Mosaic Pro with the same Apple Account, use Restore Purchases on the Scripture Mosaic Pro screen.

Backup, Export & Restore

Full backups let you save and restore your personal study library. Keep a verified backup before deleting the app or resetting your library. For help, tell us whether you are creating a backup, exporting study material, or restoring a Scripture Mosaic backup.

Bible translations

WEBU and BSB are available with the free app; Pro adds MSB and ASV and translation comparison. All included Bible editions can be read offline. When reporting an issue, include the translation name, reference, and screen involved.

Notifications

Check both Scripture Mosaic notification settings and iOS notification permission if a scheduled reminder is not appearing.

Accessibility

Scripture Mosaic supports VoiceOver, Larger Text, and Reduce Motion. If you encounter an accessibility issue, include your iOS accessibility settings and the screen or control involved.

Feedback & feature requests

Ideas are welcome. Describe the study problem you are trying to solve as well as the feature you have in mind.

Ancient texts

Related material is kept distinct.

Biblical canons differ among Christian traditions. Scripture Mosaic presents its standalone editions of 1 Enoch and Jubilees separately from its Bible translations, as historical or comparative source material. Their inclusion does not assert a single canon for every tradition.
