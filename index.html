Why Scripture Mosaic exists

Built first for my own study.

Scripture Mosaic did not begin as an app I intended to publish. I originally built it for my own personal use because I wanted a better way to study the Bible and bring my discoveries together in one place.

The Bible is rich in story, history, wisdom and truth, yet most Bible apps stop at reading, searching and basic annotation. I wanted something that could help make reading more meaningful: a place to follow ideas across Scripture, explore historical context, study significant terms, gather notes and passages, and write out my own thoughts as understanding develops over time.

As I kept building and adding features, I realized other people might find the same value in it that I have. That central idea still shapes the whole app: Scripture Mosaic is meant to help you go beyond reading and develop a lasting body of personal study, reflection and biblical research.

Common questions

Designed for deeper study.

Can I read the Bible offline?

Yes. The included Bible editions are available for offline reading. StoreKit transactions and external source websites require connectivity.

Which Bible editions are included?

WEBU (World English Bible Updated) and BSB (Berean Standard Bible) are included free. Pro adds MSB and ASV and translation comparison. Included books may differ by edition.

Is Scripture Mosaic a subscription?

No. The core experience is free. Scripture Mosaic Pro is an optional one-time lifetime purchase.

Can I research 1 Enoch and Jubilees?

Yes, with Pro. Standalone ancient-text editions are presented separately from Bible translations. Their inclusion does not assert a uniform biblical canon across Christian traditions.

Can I export a Bible study or sermon outline?

Pro Deep Studies can be exported as PDF, Word, Excel, Rich Text, Plain Text and Markdown. Use your own observations and research to develop teaching or study material.

Will my study library transfer when I reinstall?

Personal study content is stored locally. Keep a verified backup outside the app before deleting it or resetting your library. Restore Purchases restores eligible Pro access, not your personal study content.

Which devices are supported?

Scripture Mosaic is designed for iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
