Scripture Mosaic: Bible Study

Study Scripture with depth, context, and focus.

A privacy-minded, offline-first Bible study experience for iPhone—built for reading, reflection, comparison, and deeper exploration without turning your study life into an online profile.

Explore the study experienceSupport
Study tools

From reading to deeper research.

Read & annotate

Read Scripture offline in WEBU and BSB, save verses, choose from seven highlight colors, add notes and tags, organize prayers and reflections, and manage your personal library. Core reading and annotation tools are free.

Search & discover

Find passages by reference, keyword, or phrase, and discover new study ideas through the interactive Search constellation. Pro adds side-by-side translation comparison and two additional offline Bible editions: MSB and ASV.

Deep Studies & reports · Pro

Bring passages, observations, interpretation, application, notes, prayers, reflections, and research together in a dedicated workspace. Export structured studies as PDF, Word, Excel, Rich Text, Plain Text, or Markdown.

Book context · Pro

Explore in-depth book context, including authorship, historical setting, themes, structure, timelines, manuscripts, and canon traditions, with source references.

Connections & key terms · Pro

Explore Scripture connections, selected Hebrew and Greek terms, and historical parallels with 1 Enoch and Jubilees. Ancient texts are presented separately from the Bible.

Biblical Guidance · Pro

Explore curated Scripture passages for faith, relationships, hardship, purpose, and everyday life. Change translations while reading and connect relevant passages to your personal studies.

Your library & backups

Manage saved verses, highlights, notes, tags, prayers, reflections, and collections. Create and restore full backups, with additional export and backup options available through Pro.

Privacy by design

No required account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no server-side cloud sync at launch.

Your study belongs to you

Offline-first by design.

Notes, highlights, tags, reflections, prayers, reading state, preferences, Deep Studies, and related personal study content are stored locally on your device.
