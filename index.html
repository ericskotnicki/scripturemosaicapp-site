Discover the depth of Scripture.

Study tools

From reading to deeper research.

Read & annotate

Read Scripture offline in WEBU and BSB, save verses, choose from seven highlight colors, add notes and tags, organize prayers and reflections, and manage your personal library. Core reading and annotation tools are free.

Search & discover

Find passages by reference, keyword, or phrase, and discover new study ideas through the interactive Search constellation. Pro adds side-by-side translation comparison and two additional offline Bible editions: MSB and ASV.

A lifetime of study, connected

Manage your personal study library in one place. Explore study activity, Scripture coverage and content counts; search, filter and organize saved verses, highlights, notes, tags, prayers, reflections and collections. Create and restore full backups, with additional export and backup options available through Pro.

Deep Studies & reports · Pro

Bring passages, observations, interpretation, application, notes, prayers, reflections, and research together in a dedicated workspace for personal Bible studies, sermon preparation and teaching. Export structured reports as PDF, Word, Excel, Rich Text, Plain Text, or Markdown.

Book context · Pro

Explore in-depth book context, including authorship, historical setting, themes, structure, timelines, manuscripts, and canon traditions, with source references.

Connections & key terms · Pro

Explore Scripture connections, selected Hebrew and Greek terms, and historical parallels with 1 Enoch and Jubilees. Standalone ancient-text editions are presented separately from the Bible translations.

Biblical Guidance · Pro

Explore curated Scripture passages for faith, relationships, hardship, purpose, and everyday life. Change translations while reading and connect relevant passages to your personal studies.

Privacy by design

No required account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no server-side cloud sync at launch.
