Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026 · Updated November 26, 2026

Your personal Bible study belongs to you. Scripture Mosaic stores your study information on your device, requires no account, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Your study information

Your saved verses, highlights, notes, tags, reflections, prayers, reading progress, preferences, Deep Studies and related study content are stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to Scripture Mosaic. The app does not provide cloud synchronization.

Backups, exports and sharing

You can create backups and export study content. You choose where those files are saved and whether to share them. If you save or share a file using another app or service, that service’s privacy practices apply to the copy you provide.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled on your device. You can change or disable them in the app or in your iPhone settings.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Scripture Mosaic Pro lifetime purchase through the App Store. Scripture Mosaic does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles purchase and transaction information under its own privacy policy.

Support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to share. We use this information to respond to your request, resolve issues and maintain support records. Our email service provider processes these communications to provide the email service.

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information or private study content unless it is needed to resolve your request.

Website and external links

This website does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Our website hosting provider records technical information, including IP addresses, for security purposes. External websites you choose to visit have their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your study information remains on your device until you delete it, replace it by restoring a backup, or remove the app and its local data. Backup and export files saved elsewhere must be deleted separately.

Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, provide continued support or meet applicable obligations. You may contact us to request access to or deletion of information you have shared with us, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if our privacy practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@scripturemosaicapp.com.
